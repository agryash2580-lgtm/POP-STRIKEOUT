What should I look out for?

Your watchlist stays in this browser. It is never automatically sent to AI.

Phone checks ignore spaces, parentheses, dashes and dots; include the country code if that is how your number appears. Usernames match text exactly, ignoring letter case. Checks cover rendered text nodes on the current page, not the whole internet or text inside images.

Evidence, kept together.

Capture from the Pop toolbar on the page you want to save. Records stay in this browser until you explicitly export or share them.

Capture times use your device clock. Screenshots document what you saw; they do not authenticate authorship or prove an incident.

Review before saving.

Drag across the screenshot to draw opaque redactions. Saved images contain the redactions permanently.

Keyboard alternative: enter a rectangle in image pixels and choose Add redaction. On a small screen, displayed coordinates scale to the original image.

Ask Pop.

Messages and recent conversation go through STRIKEOUT to Gemini. No automatic page reading. Don't send passwords, OTPs or recovery codes. History lasts for this browser session.

AI unavailable? Start here.

Account compromised: use the platform's official recovery page, secure your email account and review active sessions. Never pay an unsolicited recovery agent.

Suspicious payment: contact your bank or payment provider using its official app or a verified contact. Preserve relevant messages and receipts.

Impersonation: capture the profile address and visible evidence, then use the platform's impersonation-reporting flow.

A second pair of eyes.

You're in charge.

Warnings cannot recall information already sent. Password fields are ignored. Pop has no sounds or unsolicited quips; jokes happen when you pet him or ask for help.

Connect AI chat

Only chat messages, recent conversation and an optional reviewed address are sent. Watchlists and evidence are not attached. Our server does not save chat bodies; the provider has its own data policies.

Use HTTPS, or localhost for development. Changing the server disconnects chat.

Local data

Watchlist and preferences use extension storage. Evidence uses the extension's local database. This is not encrypted storage or a cloud backup. Export before uninstalling. Deleting here does not erase exports or anything you already shared.
